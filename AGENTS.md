# Residents

- Shell: `fish`.
- Never undo unrelated worktree changes; they may be concurrent work.
- Use `aube` as the package manager.
- Use TanStack Start, Solid, and `ssr: 'data-only'`.
- Database: Cloudflare D1 (SQLite). Deployment: Cloudflare Workers.
- webapp is mobile first, desktop second
- Never seed production with sandbox users, votes, or phone numbers from the owners' chat.
- WhatsApp verifies a phone number; building administrators independently verify residency.
- Residents may be approved before their apartment number is known.
